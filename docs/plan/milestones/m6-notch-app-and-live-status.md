- Fixed after San tested (2026-11-25): the body went off-centre while its width sprang. The right edge overshot while the left edge crawled, up to 10 points behind for a quarter of a second. - [x] Drop-and-save, text only (slice 3, branch `m6-drop-text`, `app/MindDump/Capture/DropModel.swift`): drag highlighted text (or a link from a browser) onto the notch and let go. - [x] Slice 6, the keyboard rule (branch `m6-keyboard-rules`): the capsule has the keyboard only when the user gave it. The rule lives in one place, `IslandModel`: every open says who opened it (`OpenReason`: `user` or `system`), and `hasKeyboard` is "open, and opened by the user". `AppDelegate` only carries it out (`makeKey`, or `resignKey`). A text drop that fails (backend down, or a 422) opens the capsule with `system`: the text in the box and the reason under it, the window not key, so the app the user is in keeps the keyboard; the glass shows the non-key shade and no caret. A waiting capsule ignores clicks in other apps (that click was for the other app, and closing would throw away the words) and closes by a click on the notch. A capsule that has the keyboard and loses it closes like a click elsewhere (words cleared, a streaming answer cancelled): the window's own `didResignKeyNotification` drives it, with `NSWorkspace.didActivateApplicationNotification` as a second signal for another app becoming active. Our own close resigns too, but by then the capsule is closed, so nothing loops. Closed, a pill, a peek and a drop target never hold the keyboard.

- [x] Slice 8, motion tunings (branch `m6-motion`): San asked for morphs with a much more pronounced spring curve and more blur, so the island stops reading as a resize. There are now three complete sets of springs in `IslandMotion.swift`, switchable while the app runs: `calm` (the slice 1 values, islandcn's iPhone measurements, unchanged), `springy` (every shape change overshoots about 2 to 6% and settles, springs about 10% longer, blur on crossing content roughly doubled) and `drastic` (it pushes clearly past, about 7 to 15%, and eases back, springs 20 to 30% longer so the bounce reads as mass, blur peaks of 14 to 24 points). Default `springy`, one constant (`IslandMotion.defaultTuning`). `MD_MOTION_TUNING=calm|springy|drastic` at launch; debug builds switch it live with `notifyutil -p io.github.sxnfer.MindDump.debug.tuning-<name>` or the script verb `tuning <name>`, and log `[motion] tuning <name>`. A switch applies from the next transition. Reduce Motion ignores the tuning (it keeps the 0.2 s ease with no blur).

- What else changed, and why. (1) A pill that changes in place ("Saved" to "Reading page") blurs its glyph and words once while its width springs (5 points in springy, 9 in drastic, none in calm), so the words re-form instead of sliding out with the edge. (2) Content inside the glass blurs at most 12 points in every tuning (`glassBlurLimit`, the most calm uses there): with 18 to 24 points in the glass, a drop release went from 1 late frame in three runs to 6, its second leg from 0 to 4, closes from 6 to 9 and 11. The black body's content keeps the full blur.
