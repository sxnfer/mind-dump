# D17: Licence

Status: Approved, 2026-11-17
Problem: people need to know what they may do with the code. With no licence, nobody may legally reuse it, even in a public repo.
Without a tool: the code is "all rights reserved" by default.
Options: MIT; Apache-2.0; AGPL-3.0.
Chosen: MIT.
Why: short, well known, and easy to read. Anyone may use, change and share the code if they keep the notice. Fits an app that each person runs on their own Mac.
Cost: no patent grant (Apache-2.0 has one), and others may close-source a fork.
We will switch if: a contributor or company needs an explicit patent grant. Then move to Apache-2.0.
